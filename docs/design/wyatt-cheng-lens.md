# Design lens: Wyatt Cheng's principles (Oct 30)

**What this is:** a review lens for Hollowmere design decisions, distilled from Wyatt Cheng's *public* statements (tweets, interviews, a GDC talk, and press write-ups of his short videos). It is **not** him and doesn't speak for him. Use it as a second opinion: "how would a veteran Blizzard systems designer poke at this?"

**Who he is:** ex-Blizzard designer, 2003 onward: WoW raid bosses (Prophet Skeram, Twin Emperors, Patchwerk, Four Horsemen), Diablo III class/skill/item design and live patch lead, game director of Diablo Immortal. Now Senior Design Director at Second Dinner (Marvel Snap). Posts as **candlesan** on YouTube (shorts and long-form), TikTok, Instagram, X and Twitch.

**Source gap:** Instagram and TikTok reels can't be watched from here. Everything below comes from text sources. To deepen the lens, paste transcripts of his YouTube @candlesan videos/shorts and fold them in.

## The principles

| # | Principle | Where it comes from | The question it asks |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Players see features, not intent.** They take the path of least resistance, whatever the tuning meant. | D3 Auction House thread (2021) | "What will players *actually* do here?" |
| 2 | **Solve the most important problems first.** Don't geek out on details. | GDC 2013, "Through the Grinder" | "Is this the biggest open problem, or the most fun one to think about?" |
| 3 | **Test the whole game, for a long time, and keep buffer for pivots.** | D3 thread: public testing stopped at Skeleton King | "Has anyone played this end to end?" |
| 4 | **Fun with templates ≠ fun with real builds.** | D3 PvP | "Have we tested the strange builds?" |
| 5 | **Put hard content in the right place.** Lowest difficulty = least skilled player in worst gear. | D3 → Greater Rifts | "Who is this difficulty for, and can they choose it?" |
| 6 | **Don't design to a number.** | D3 skill runes | "Is this count serving the content, or a template?" |
| 7 | **Rarity makes moments.** | Immortal drop rates | "Will getting this feel special?" |
| 8 | **The best way to get stronger is playing the game.** | Immortal interviews | "Is there a shortcut that bypasses the core loop?" |
| 9 | **A hub with people makes the world feel alive.** | D3 hub cut; Immortal Westmarch | "Does the hub feel inhabited and changing?" |
| 10 | **The platform is design space.** | Immortal: touch control → Icy Ground | "What does our input make possible?" |
| 11 | **Puzzles leak.** Fix bugs, accept being outsmarted. | WoW Skeram | "How could a player cheat this mechanic's information?" |
| 12 | **Game feel is many small things.** Camera shake is underrated. | His posts on combat feel | "Does the core action feel good with nothing else?" |
| 13 | **XP curves grow linearly or quadratically, not exponentially.** | His "Did you know?" post | "What does our level curve look like on a graph?" |
| 14 | **Name things precisely.** | D3 thread | "Does our wording say what we mean?" |
| 15 | **Talk about fun with a vocabulary** (8 Kinds of Fun). | His shorts | "Which 2–3 kinds of fun is this feature for?" |

## Review checklist
1. Path of least resistance: what's the easiest dominant strategy?
2. Priority: is this the most important unsolved problem?
3. Real builds: what's the weirdest combination that hits this?
4. Placement: is the difficulty where the right player meets it?
5. Numbers: are any counts fixed by habit?
6. Feel: does it feel good in a greybox?
7. Leaks: can the player get around it via UI, systems or info?
8. Fun type: which kinds of fun does it serve?
