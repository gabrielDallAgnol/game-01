# AI lab: the Chronicler on real run logs (Oct 19)

**How:** `tools/ai-lab.mjs` plays a 12-night run (bot on autopilot, Story difficulty) and records the exact prompts the game would send: every encounter rewrite, one invented side quest per dawn, and a "previously on" recap. Two agents on the quick model tier answered them as the in-game model would. `tools/ai-lab-check.mjs` then ran the game's own parsers and `story.invent` on the answers.

## Results
| Check | Run 1 (Rio) | Run 2 (Berlin) |
|---|---|---|
| Rewrites valid | 22/22 | 19/19 |
| Words per scene | avg 47, max 60 (3 beats) | avg 39, max 49 (2–3 beats) |
| Side quests valid and placed | 11/11 | 11/11 |
| Templates used | mystery 2, rescue 3, recruit 2, echo 2, bargain 1, dilemma 1 | mystery 3, rescue 3, recruit 2, echo 1, bargain 1, dilemma 1 |
| Recap valid | 1/1 | 1/1 |

## What reads well
| Example | Why it works |
|---|---|
| "One lamp burns again": the north-road lamp is lit again, with a small handprint in the soot | Pays off the day-2 Mark |
| "The boy at the gate": the bitten boy you treated comes back and says *something is counting us* | Pays off the teacher's secret |
| "The girl who drew the flag" | Pays off the school drawings |
| "A stranger at the west gate" | Comes after you paid the Bridge Crew's toll |
| A rider under your flag on the overpass | Comes from leaving the flag up |
| Recaps | Name the companions you actually have and end on what still waits ("the woman you answered on Channel 9 still waits") |

## Problems found and fixed
| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| Text promised more than the effect gave ("a dozen children" for +2 people) | The menu now shows each choice's real effect; the prompt says the words must match it |
| Scenes were too long for a phone (was ≤75 words) | 2–3 beats of ≤22 words, one **bold** phrase, shown one page at a time |

## Still open
- The lab does not feed invented quests back into the run, so a quest never calls back to an invented quest. The live game does, through the chronicle.
- Invented characters show up only on the 'recruit' template as companions. Rescue and dilemma people stay names in the story; a future "they come back" echo could recruit them.
