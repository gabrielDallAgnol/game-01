# Research: base building, Red Alert 2, best sellers, resources, pacing (Oct 18 night)

## Red Alert 2
- Ore fields (gems ≈2×), regrow; income lives away from the base → you defend routes. Tech buildings captured by engineers give *different kinds* of bonus: Oil Derrick (money trickle), Hospital (heal), Outpost (repair/defence), Airport (paradrop). Build radius + MCV redeploy = deliberate, risky expansion. Power: low power shuts down best defences and radar — one visible failure state.
- Readability: team colour on everything, distinct silhouettes, buildings unfold from the ground, EVA voice lines for each state change.

## Resources (max 4 + 1 meter)
Recommended set "Lights On": Scrap (replaces gold), Food, Survivors, Power; Heat is a meter, not a resource. Generators give power but noise; a silent Substation landmark is a reason to explore. Brownout shuts the best defences first.

## Procedural city
District grammar (12×12 blocks: residential, commercial, industrial, park, civic, transit) → per-block rarity roll 60/28/10/2 (common/rare/unique/legendary, pity rule) → WFC detail with path validation. Grow in rings per dawn/claim; telegraph rarity through the shroud. 3–5 nests that are real buildings (metro, mall, hospital, stadium as the endgame).

## Pacing
Heat 0–100 decaying 2%/s toward a floor (survivors × 0.5); generators +3/s, shots +0.5, homes/landmarks raise the floor; 40 → wanderers converge, 70 → horde with 60 s warning, 100 → double horde. L4D director: build-up / peak / fade / relax (30–45 s). Difficulty presets as bundles (threat ×, economy ×, heat decay, adaptation, death rule). First 10 minutes: safe ring 0, guaranteed nearby rare block, night 1 one direction 6–8 slow dead, one new system per night. Autopilot: unlockable, 70% yield, auto-pauses on key decisions.
